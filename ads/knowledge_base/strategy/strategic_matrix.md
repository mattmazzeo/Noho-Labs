# Strategic Opportunity Matrix & 90-Day Roadmap

## 1. SWOT Analysis (Noho Labs & Aluna)

### Strengths
- **Clean Slate:** No prior Meta Ads history means no policy violations or shadowbans to overcome.
- **Dual-Brand Architecture:** Aluna (supplements) and Noho Labs (telehealth/Rx) allows for regulatory arbitrage and diversified risk.
- **Market Timing:** Entering right as the peptide market transitions from niche biohacking to mainstream wellness.
- **Brand Aesthetic:** Professional, clean branding positions well against sketchy "research peptide" competitors.

### Weaknesses
- **No Existing Data:** Starting from scratch on pixel data, audience building, and creative testing.
- **High Friction Category:** Telehealth/Rx funnels naturally have higher drop-off rates than pure e-commerce.
- **Compliance Burden:** Navigating FDA Category 2 uncertainties and Meta's Tier 2/3 health restrictions.
- **Budget Requirements:** High production quality needed to compete with well-funded incumbents (Rho, Hims).

### Opportunities
- **Sermorelin/Growth Hormone:** Massively under-advertised category (~2,600 ads) with high consumer interest.
- **BPC-157 / Recovery:** The "Wolverine Stack" is beloved by the community but lacks a premium, compliant DTC brand.
- **Protocol-Based Selling:** Moving away from "buy this vial" to "subscribe to this protocol" (increases AOV and retention).
- **Influencer Whitelisting:** The proven playbook in this space (Rho model) is highly replicable with the right partners.
- **GLP-1 Off-Ramping:** Creating protocols for the millions of patients looking to maintain muscle/energy while tapering off weight-loss drugs.

### Threats
- **FDA Enforcement:** Potential crackdowns on compounding pharmacies or shifting Category 1/2 statuses.
- **Meta Policy Changes:** Further tightening of health advertising rules or arbitrary account bans.
- **Incumbent Spending:** Hims/Ro driving up CPMs in the broader telehealth space.
- **Supply Chain:** API sourcing bottlenecks if demand outpaces high-quality compounding capacity.

## 2. Brand Positioning Strategy

### Aluna Science (The Supplement Play)
**The Goal:** Maximum reach, lowest friction, highest volume.
**The Positioning:** "Cellular Longevity Essentials."
**The Competitor to Beat:** Rho Nutrition, Cymbiotika.
**The Hero Products:** Liposomal NAD+, Liposomal Glutathione, Oral BPC-157 (if compliant).
**The Meta Strategy:** 
- No age-gating required.
- Heavy influencer whitelisting (Tier 2/3 wellness creators).
- UGC video focus ("I added this to my routine").
- "Supports" language.
- Bundle-first pricing strategy (e.g., "The Aluna Longevity Stack").

### Noho Labs (The Telehealth/Rx Play)
**The Goal:** Highest authority, highest LTV, premium patient experience.
**The Positioning:** "Your Concierge Peptide Protocol."
**The Competitor to Beat:** AgelessRx, local wellness clinics.
**The Hero Products:** Sermorelin, Injectable NAD+, GHK-Cu, specialized peptide stacks.
**The Meta Strategy:**
- Age-gated ads (required for Rx).
- Physician-led content and educational deep-dives.
- Focus on specific outcomes (recovery, sleep, lean mass).
- Lead generation → Consultation funnel.
- Target higher-income demographics (Performance Optimizers).

## 3. The 90-Day Launch Roadmap

### Month 1: Infrastructure & Compliance Foundation

**Technical Setup:**
- [ ] Implement Conversions API (CAPI) for both domains (critical for Tier 2/3 Meta accounts).
- [ ] Set up intermediary landing pages (advertorials) to buffer main domains.
- [ ] Configure Meta Business Manager, Ad Accounts, and apply for Pharmacy/Telehealth authorization.

**Creative Production (Batch 1):**
- [ ] Source 5 UGC creators to film 20 "Hook + Problem + Solution" videos for Aluna (NAD+/Glutathione).
- [ ] Film 5 physician-led educational videos for Noho Labs (Sermorelin/Recovery).
- [ ] Design 10 static bundle/product shots with strong social proof elements.

**Funnel Optimization:**
- [ ] Implement "Bundle First" UI on Aluna product pages.
- [ ] Build seamless Quiz → Consult flow for Noho Labs.
- [ ] Set up automated email/SMS abandon cart sequences.

### Month 2: The "Sandbox" Testing Phase

**Launch Strategy (Aluna):**
- **Budget:** $100-$150/day.
- **Campaign Structure:** 1 Broad Audience Campaign (Advantage+ Shopping) + 1 Retargeting Campaign.
- **Testing:** 5 different hooks per product. Kill any ad with <1% outbound CTR after $50 spend.
- **Goal:** Identify 1-2 winning creative formats and establish baseline CPA.

**Launch Strategy (Noho Labs):**
- **Budget:** $50-$100/day.
- **Campaign Structure:** Lead Generation (In-platform or Landing Page).
- **Testing:** Educational vs. Symptom-focused messaging.
- **Goal:** Establish Cost Per Consult and Consult-to-Rx conversion rate.

**Influencer Outreach:**
- [ ] Contact 15-20 Tier 3/4 influencers for UGC and potential whitelisting.
- [ ] Secure 3 initial whitelisting partners.

### Month 3: Scaling & Whitelisting Integration

**Scale the Winners:**
- Increase budget by 20% every 3 days on winning Aluna creatives.
- Shift 60% of Aluna budget to Influencer Whitelisting campaigns.
- Introduce "Stack/Protocol" messaging based on winning individual products.

**Refine the Funnel:**
- Implement A/B testing on Noho Labs quiz (length vs. intent).
- Launch "Retargeting with Authority" (showing physician videos to people who abandoned cart).
- Introduce subscription upsells post-purchase.

**Metrics to Hit by Day 90:**
- **Aluna:** Blended CAC < $80, AOV > $120.
- **Noho Labs:** Cost Per Consult < $150, Rx Conversion > 60%.
- **Overall:** 3 active whitelisting partners driving 30%+ of revenue.

## 4. Budget Allocation Model (First $50K)

If you have $50,000 to launch, here is the recommended allocation:

| Category | Allocation | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aluna Media Spend** | 40% | $20,000 | Focus on immediate e-commerce conversions |
| **Noho Labs Media Spend** | 20% | $10,000 | Focus on lead gen / consults (higher friction) |
| **Creative Production** | 20% | $10,000 | UGC creators, video editing, static design |
| **Influencer Fees** | 10% | $5,000 | Flat fees for whitelisting rights (Tier 3/4) |
| **Tech/Infrastructure** | 10% | $5,000 | CAPI setup, landing page software, tracking |

### Why this allocation?
Creative is the single biggest variable in Meta Ads success in 2026. Underfunding creative to overfund media spend is the most common mistake new advertisers make. You need a constant pipeline of new hooks to beat ad fatigue.

## 5. Final Strategic Recommendation

The peptide market is at an inflection point. The "wild west" era of research chemical sites is ending, and the era of compliant, brand-forward, protocol-based longevity companies is beginning.

Your dual-brand strategy is your biggest advantage. 

Use **Aluna** to acquire customers cheaply through low-friction supplements (NAD+, Glutathione). Once they are in your ecosystem and trust the brand, cross-sell them into **Noho Labs** for higher-tier, higher-LTV prescription protocols (Sermorelin, advanced recovery stacks) when they need more powerful interventions.

Start with Aluna on Meta Ads. The compliance burden is lower, the pixel optimization is better, and you can generate cash flow faster while building the more complex Noho Labs telehealth funnel in the background.
