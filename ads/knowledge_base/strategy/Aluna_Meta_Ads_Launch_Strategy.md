# Aluna: Meta Ads Launch Strategy (Injectable NAD+)

**Goal:** Launch Aluna's injectable NAD+ offering on Meta, aggressively acquire customers using our structural pricing advantage, and build a subscriber base of 2,000–5,000 patients ahead of the July 2026 peptide reclassification.

**The Structural Advantage:** System Labs and other competitors rely on third-party pharmacies, marking up costs to cover wholesale prices. Because Aluna is vertically integrated with a compounding pharmacy, our fully loaded COGS for a 5mL vial is ~$53. This allows us to offer System Labs' "promotional" Month 1 price ($79) as our permanent everyday price while maintaining profitability.

## 3. Compliance & Account Setup Framework

As a telehealth brand selling prescription injectables, Aluna will fall under Meta's restricted healthcare policies.

### Technical Requirements
1. **Age-Gating:** All ad sets must be restricted to 18+.
2. **Meta Telehealth Certification:** Apply for Meta's online pharmacy/telehealth certification immediately to prevent account bans.
3. **Conversions API (CAPI):** Mandatory. Meta restricts pixel data for health brands. Server-side tracking via CAPI ensures we capture purchase events to train the algorithm.
4. **Intermediary Domain:** Send ad traffic to an advertorial or educational landing page (e.g., `tryaluna.com/nad-science`) rather than directly to the checkout cart. This warms the lead and satisfies Meta's policy against direct-to-cart Rx advertising.

### Creative Compliance Rules
- **DO NOT** make explicit medical claims ("Cures chronic fatigue").
- **DO** use structural/functional language ("Supports cellular energy," "Enhances recovery").
- **DO NOT** show needles entering skin or blood.
- **DO** show the premium vial, the unboxing experience, and healthy people living actively.

## 5. Campaign Structure

We will use a simplified, modern Meta Ads account structure to consolidate learning.

### Campaign 1: The Sandbox (Testing)
- **Objective:** Sales (Conversions)
- **Budget:** 20% of total spend ($100/day)
- **Audience:** Broad (18+, licensed states only)
- **Structure:** 1 Ad Set. Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) enabled. Test 3 videos, 2 images, 2 primary texts, and 2 headlines per test.
- **Goal:** Identify winning creative angles. Kill anything under 1% CTR after $50 spent.

### Campaign 2: The Scale (Winners)
- **Objective:** Sales (Conversions)
- **Budget:** 80% of total spend ($400/day)
- **Audience:** Advantage+ Shopping Campaign (ASC) restricted to licensed states.
- **Structure:** Only move proven winners from the Sandbox into this campaign. Let Meta allocate budget to the best performers.
