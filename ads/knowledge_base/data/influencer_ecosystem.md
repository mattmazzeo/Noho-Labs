## 3. Whitelisting Strategy (How to Execute)

### What is Whitelisting?
Running paid ads through an influencer's Facebook/Instagram page instead of your brand page. The ad appears as if the influencer posted it organically, dramatically increasing trust and CTR.

### Setup Requirements:
1. Influencer grants "Advertiser Access" to your Business Manager
2. Or: Use Meta's "Partnership Ads" (formerly Branded Content Ads)
3. Influencer's page must be public with Business/Creator status
4. Both parties need connected Business Manager accounts

### Best Practices (Observed from Rho's Strategy):
- Run 5-10 ad variations per influencer page
- Mix video UGC + static testimonial quotes
- Use influencer's authentic voice (don't over-script)
- Include influencer's personal discount code
- Test different hooks from same influencer content
- Rotate creative every 4-6 weeks to prevent fatigue

### Recommended Whitelisting Budget Allocation:
- 60% of ad spend through influencer/whitelisted pages
- 40% through brand page (Noho Labs / Aluna)
- Start with 3-5 influencer partners, scale to 10+

## 4. Partnership Models & Economics

### Model A: Flat Fee + Whitelisting Rights
- Pay influencer $5K-$50K for content creation
- Secure 90-day (minimum) whitelisting rights
- Brand controls ad spend and targeting
- Best for: Tier 1-2 influencers with proven audiences

### Model B: Affiliate/Revenue Share
- Give influencer unique discount code (10-20% off)
- Pay 10-20% commission on attributed sales
- Lower upfront cost, aligned incentives
- Best for: Tier 3-4 influencers, testing new partnerships

### Model C: Equity/Advisory Partnership
- Offer equity stake or advisory role
- Long-term brand ambassador commitment
- Deepest integration, highest authenticity
- Best for: Tier 1 influencers who genuinely use the product
- Example: Dr. Mark Hyman as "Rho Partner"

### Model D: UGC-Only (No Posting)
- Pay creator $500-$2K to produce video content
- Brand owns all rights, runs as ads
- No requirement for influencer to post organically
- Best for: Scaling creative volume quickly

## 5. Recommended Influencer Strategy for Noho Labs / Aluna

### Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundation
- Sign 3-5 UGC creators ($500-$2K each) for initial ad creative
- Partner with 1-2 physicians for authority content
- Secure whitelisting rights from all partners
- Budget: $10K-$25K total

### Phase 2 (Months 3-6): Scale
- Add 2-3 Tier 2 fitness/performance influencers
- Add 2-3 Tier 3 wellness/lifestyle creators
- Begin affiliate program for micro-influencers
- Budget: $25K-$75K total

### Phase 3 (Months 6-12): Authority
- Pursue 1 Tier 1 partnership (Huberman, Attia, etc.)
- Build out to 10+ active whitelisting partners
- Launch podcast sponsorship strategy
- Budget: $75K-$200K total

### Key Metrics to Track:
- Cost per whitelisted ad engagement vs. brand page ads
- Influencer-attributed revenue (discount code tracking)
- Content production velocity (videos/month)
- Whitelisted ad CTR vs. brand ad CTR (expect 2-3x improvement)
- CAC by influencer partner (identify top performers)
