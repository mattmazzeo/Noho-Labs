## KEY PATTERNS & INSIGHTS

### Creative Formats (by prevalence):
1. **UGC Video** (60-70% of ads) — Doctor testimonials, influencer reviews, founder stories
2. **Static Product Images** (15-20%) — Bundle shots, product photography
3. **Carousel** (10%) — Multiple product images, before/after concepts
4. **Long-form Story Ads** (5%) — Narrative text with images (like Velverdo dental story)

### Dominant Messaging Themes:
1. **Absorption/Bioavailability** — "Liposomal", "your body can actually use", "gets into cells"
2. **Anti-Aging/Longevity** — Cellular health, collagen loss, NAD decline
3. **Convenience vs. Alternatives** — "No needles", "no IV", "at-home", patches vs. pills
4. **Doctor/Expert Authority** — MDs, functional medicine doctors, pharmacists
5. **Risk Reversal** — 60-90 day money-back guarantees
6. **Discount/Urgency** — Limited time codes, flash sales, "ends Tuesday"
7. **Science-Backed Claims** — PubMed citations, "clinically proven", specific peptide names

### Advertising Strategies:
1. **Whitelisting/Partnership Ads** — Running brand ads through influencer pages (Rho, Seranova)
2. **Age-Gating** — Most health/telehealth ads require login to view (Meta policy)
3. **Multi-Variant Testing** — Same brand running 10-50+ active ads simultaneously
4. **Influencer Stacking** — Using 5-10+ different influencers for same brand
5. **Education-First Funnels** — Teaching about the science before selling
6. **Bundle/Stack Positioning** — Selling protocols, not individual products

### Compliance Observations:
- All telehealth brands (Hims, Ro, Beyond the Scale) are age-gated
- Supplement brands (Rho, Cymbiotika) are NOT age-gated — they use "supports" language
- Peptide therapy clinics are age-gated
- No before/after imagery visible in any compliant ads
- Language uses "supports", "helps maintain", "may help" — never "treats" or "cures"

### Pricing/Offer Patterns:
- Rho: 20-25% off first order, bundle savings ($84 off)
- Seranova: 90-day risk-free trial
- Kind Patches: "Be one of the first" (early adopter angle)
- Factor (GLP-1 adjacent): "Free Breakfast For 1 Year"
- Magnali: Urgency deadlines ("Offer Ends Tuesday")

## COMPETITIVE GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES FOR NOHO LABS / ALUNA

1. **Sermorelin is WIDE OPEN** — Only ~2,600 results, 3 advertisers visible, all age-gated. Massive opportunity.
2. **No dominant peptide telehealth brand** — Hims/Ro own GLP-1 but nobody owns the broader peptide therapy space on Meta.
3. **Education content is underserved** — Most ads go straight to product. Opportunity for "peptide education" content marketing via ads.
4. **Women's health angle** — Dr. Mary Claire Haver proves this works. Peptides for menopause, skin, bone health = underserved.
5. **Whitelisting model is proven** — Rho's success with 10+ influencer pages shows this scales. Noho/Aluna should build influencer partnerships early.
6. **"Complete protocol" positioning** — Magnali's "first complete aging system" resonates. Noho could own "complete peptide protocol."
7. **Anti-DIY messaging** — Halo Concierge's angle of "peptides shouldn't be DIY" positions legitimate telehealth as safer alternative.
8. **Liposomal/delivery innovation** — Everyone claims better absorption. Noho needs a clear delivery/quality differentiator.

| Anti-Aging/Collagen Peptides | HIGH | Dr. Mary Claire Haver, Rho, Seranova | Medium - crowded but proven |

### Top Strategic Takeaways for Noho Labs / Aluna:

1. **Rho Nutrition is the #1 model to study** — They've built a massive influencer whitelisting operation for NAD+/Glutathione supplements. ~910 active ads through 10+ influencer pages.

2. **The telehealth peptide space is WIDE OPEN on Meta** — Unlike GLP-1 (dominated by Hims/Ro), broader peptide therapy has no dominant advertiser.

3. **Age-gating is inevitable** — Any ad mentioning specific treatments will be age-gated. Plan creative strategy around this.

4. **UGC video from doctors/experts is the winning format** — Dr. Mary Claire Haver, Dr. Jeremy London, Dr. Becky Campbell all prove doctor-as-spokesperson works.

5. **"Research use only" peptide sellers are spending heavily** — Shows massive consumer demand for peptides that legitimate telehealth can capture.

6. **Whitelisting through influencer pages is the scaling mechanism** — Not just running ads from your brand page, but through 5-10+ partner pages.

7. **Education-first content wins** — Brands that explain the science (NMN→NAD conversion, collagen loss in menopause, cellular aging) outperform pure product ads.

8. **Bundle/Stack positioning drives AOV** — "Total Rho Stack", "Wolverine Stack", "Cellular Longevity Stack" — naming protocols increases perceived value.
