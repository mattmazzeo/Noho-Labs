## Key Observations

1. **They are BRAND NEW** — first ads started May 12, 2026 (5 weeks ago)
2. **Heavy first-month discount strategy** — 47-50% off first month across the board
3. **Age-gated ads** — All System Labs ads require age confirmation to view (Rx products)
4. **~37,000 results** in Meta Ad Library for "system labs NAD" (but this includes other advertisers with those keywords)
5. **Multiple active ads** — at least 6+ visible System Labs ads running simultaneously
6. **San Francisco based** — likely VC-backed given the aggressive pricing and ad spend
7. **They DON'T own their pharmacy** — "We source peptides from licensed U.S. pharmacies"
8. **NAD+ is 1000mg monthly supply** — that's a 5ml vial at 200mg/ml (same as what Noho can produce)

## Their Funnel
1. See ad (age-gated)
2. "See if you qualify" → intake form (2 minutes)
3. Clinician reviews via chat
4. Rx written → ships from partner pharmacy
5. Free 2-day shipping
6. Ongoing clinician support via messaging

## Strengths
- Beautiful brand/design (dark, premium, modern)
- Simple product line (focused)
- Aggressive first-month pricing hooks
- Fast intake (2-minute form, no appointment)
- Free 2-day shipping included
- Strong social proof (verified customer reviews)
- GLP-1 offering drives traffic

## Weaknesses
- **Don't own their pharmacy** — margin disadvantage vs. Noho/Aluna
- **Brand new** — no established trust, limited reviews
- **No stacking/bundling** — all products sold individually
- **$149/mo ongoing** for NAD+ is standard pricing — not disruptive after Month 1
- **No influencer/whitelisting strategy visible** — all ads from their own page
- **San Francisco overhead** — expensive operations base

## The Key Insight

System Labs uses a **loss-leader first month** to acquire customers, then jumps to full price ($149/mo). This creates churn risk at Month 2 when the price nearly doubles.

Aluna's advantage: Because you OWN the pharmacy, you can offer System Labs' PROMO price ($79) as your EVERYDAY price and still maintain healthy margins.

Your COGS for NAD+ 5ml: ~$53
At $79/mo ongoing: $26 gross margin (33%)
At $99/mo ongoing: $46 gross margin (46%)

System Labs COGS (buying from partner pharmacy): likely $75-$100+ per vial
At $79 first month: They're LOSING money on Month 1
At $149 ongoing: They need the markup to cover pharmacy costs + margin
