# Funnel & Conversion Architecture Teardowns

## 1. Rho Nutrition (Supplement DTC - Best-in-Class)

### Funnel Architecture:
```
Meta Ad (UGC Video/Static) → Product Page or Collection Page → Add to Cart → Checkout
                                                                      ↓
                                                              Subscription Upsell
```

### Landing Page Elements:
- **Hero:** "Choose Your Liquid Vitamin Therapy™" with lifestyle imagery
- **Trust Bar:** "60-DAY GUARANTEE" + "20K+ 5-STAR REVIEWS" + "FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS $150+"
- **Navigation:** Shop Bestsellers | Shop By Benefit | Bundles (SAVE 10-20% OFF) | Learn | Support
- **Product Grid:** Shows bundles first (higher AOV), then individual products

### Conversion Optimization Tactics:
1. **Bundle-first display** — Bundles shown before individual products (drives AOV)
2. **Review counts on every product** — 8,812 reviews on hero product creates massive social proof
3. **Savings callouts** — "SAVE $19.48" shown on every bundle
4. **Star ratings visible** — 4.7-4.8 stars across all products
5. **"BESTSELLER" / "NEW" / "SOLD OUT" badges** — Creates urgency and validation
6. **Subscription option** — Save additional % on recurring orders
7. **Free shipping threshold** — $150+ (encourages bundle purchase)
8. **FAQ section** — Addresses objections (storage, testing, allergens, comparison to injections)
9. **Authority testimonials** — Dr. Mark Hyman quote on collection page
10. **Email capture** — Newsletter signup for retention

### Product Page Structure (Cellular Longevity Essentials - 8,812 reviews):
- Product images (multiple angles)
- Price: ~~$148.85~~ → $133.97 (SAVE $14.88)
- Bundle contents: NAD+ + Curcumin + Resveratrol + Glutathione
- Subscription option with additional savings
- "Add to Cart" CTA
- Benefits section
- How to use
- Ingredients/supplement facts
- Reviews section with photos

### Key Metrics (Estimated):
- AOV: $100-$150 (bundle-driven)
- Subscription rate: 30-40% (estimated from subscription infrastructure)
- Conversion rate: 2.5-4% (typical for well-optimized DTC supplement)
- LTV: $300-$500 (subscription model)

## 2. AgelessRx (Telehealth - Longevity)

### Funnel Architecture:
```
Meta Ad (Age-Gated) → Landing Page → Quiz/Assessment → Doctor Consultation → Prescription → Pharmacy Fulfillment
```

### Key Funnel Elements:
- Age-gated ads (limits reach but ensures compliance)
- Quiz-based intake (personalizes the experience)
- Physician consultation (required for Rx products)
- Subscription model for ongoing prescriptions
- Products: NAD+ injections, rapamycin, metformin, peptides

### Estimated Metrics:
- CAC: $150-$300 (higher due to age-gating limiting reach)
- AOV: $200-$500 (Rx products command premium)
- LTV: $1,000-$3,000 (ongoing prescriptions)
- Consultation fee: $99-$199

## 3. Cymbiotika (Premium Supplement DTC)

### Funnel Architecture:
```
Meta Ad (UGC/Brand) → Product Page → Add to Cart → Checkout
                  ↓
          Retail (Ulta/Target) → In-Store Purchase
```

### Landing Page Elements:
- Premium/luxury aesthetic (dark backgrounds, gold accents)
- "100+ Million Packets Sold" social proof
- "50,000+ Reviews" trust signal
- Retail availability (Ulta, Target) as credibility
- Subscription-first pricing model
- Science/ingredient education pages

### Dual-Channel Strategy:
- DTC (cymbiotika.com) — Higher margin, subscription-focused
- Retail (Ulta, Target) — Awareness, credibility, new customer acquisition
- Meta Ads drive both channels simultaneously

### Estimated Metrics:
- AOV: $60-$90 (individual products)
- Subscription rate: 40-50% (subscription pricing incentive)
- Conversion rate: 2-3%
- LTV: $400-$800

## Key Implications for Noho Labs / Aluna:

### The Friction Paradox:
- **Supplement brands** (Rho, Cymbiotika) have LOW friction funnels → higher conversion rates, lower CAC
- **Telehealth brands** (AgelessRx, Hims) have HIGH friction funnels → lower conversion rates, higher CAC, but higher LTV
- **Noho Labs needs to decide:** Are you selling supplements (low friction) or prescriptions (high friction)?

### Recommended Funnel Architecture for Noho Labs:
```
Meta Ad (Education/UGC) → Symptom Quiz Landing Page → Personalized Protocol Recommendation → Book Consultation → Physician Visit → Prescription + Fulfillment → Ongoing Subscription
```

### Recommended Funnel Architecture for Aluna (if supplement brand):
```
Meta Ad (UGC/Influencer) → Product Page → Add to Cart → Checkout → Subscription Upsell
```

### Critical Conversion Elements to Include:
1. **Social proof** — Review counts, customer counts, before/after labs
2. **Risk reversal** — Money-back guarantee (60-90 days)
3. **Authority** — Physician endorsements, clinical data
4. **Urgency** — Limited-time offers, discount codes
5. **Subscription incentive** — 10-20% off recurring orders
6. **Free shipping threshold** — Drives AOV up
7. **Bundle/stack options** — Pre-built protocols at a discount
