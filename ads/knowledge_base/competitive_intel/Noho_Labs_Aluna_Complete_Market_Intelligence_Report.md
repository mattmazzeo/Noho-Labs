## Executive Summary

The peptide and longevity market is experiencing an unprecedented inflection point. Driven by the mainstreaming of GLP-1 receptor agonists (projected to reach 25 million US patients by 2030) and surging consumer interest in healthspan extension, the global peptide therapeutics market is expanding at a 7-13% CAGR toward a $160B+ valuation.

For Noho Labs and Aluna Science, this presents a massive opportunity — provided you navigate the complex regulatory and advertising landscape correctly. Meta's November 2025 health advertising restrictions and the FDA's ongoing evaluation of Category 1 and 2 compounding substances have created a high-friction environment that favors sophisticated, compliant operators over "wild west" research chemical vendors.

This report synthesizes data across market sizing, 29 competitor profiles, Similarweb traffic analytics, Meta Ad Library quantitative analysis, creative teardowns, funnel architecture, audience psychographics, regulatory compliance, and influencer ecosystem mapping to deliver a comprehensive 90-day launch roadmap.

**The core recommendation:** Leverage your dual-brand architecture. Use **Aluna Science** (supplements) for low-friction, high-volume customer acquisition via influencer whitelisting, and use **Noho Labs** (telehealth/Rx) to capture high-LTV patients seeking physician-supervised, premium peptide protocols. Acquire customers cheaply through Aluna, then cross-sell into Noho Labs for higher-tier interventions.

### Messaging Architecture Patterns

**Pattern 1: "Science + Simplicity" (Rho Nutrition)**

**Pattern 2: "Routine Integration" (Influencer Whitelisting)**

This pattern drives AOV, positions the brand as a complete solution, and creates switching costs.

## 6. Funnel & Conversion Architecture

The fundamental tension in health advertising is the **"Friction Paradox."** Supplement brands have low-friction funnels (3-4 clicks to purchase) resulting in higher conversion rates and lower CAC. Telehealth brands have high-friction funnels (quiz → consult → Rx) resulting in lower conversion rates and higher CAC, but significantly higher LTV.

### Funnel Comparison Matrix

| Brand | Funnel Type | Steps to Purchase | Avg. Time to Convert | Friction Level | Est. CVR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rho Nutrition | Direct e-commerce | 3-4 clicks | Minutes | Low | 2.5-4% |
| Cymbiotika | Direct e-commerce | 3-4 clicks | Minutes | Low | 2-3% |
| Kind Patches | Direct e-commerce | 2-3 clicks | Minutes | Very Low | 3-5% |
| Hims | Telehealth + Rx | 4-5 steps | Hours-Days | Medium | 5-8% (quiz-to-sub) |
| AgelessRx | Telehealth + Rx | 5-7 steps | Days | High | 1-3% |

### Rho Nutrition Funnel Teardown (Best-in-Class Supplement)

**Architecture:** Meta Ad (UGC Video/Static) → Product/Collection Page → Add to Cart → Checkout → Subscription Upsell

**Key Conversion Elements:**
1. Bundle-first display (bundles shown before individual products to drive AOV)
2. Massive review counts (8,812 reviews on hero product)
3. Savings callouts ("SAVE $19.48" on every bundle)
4. Star ratings visible (4.7-4.8 stars across all products)
5. Urgency badges ("BESTSELLER" / "NEW" / "SOLD OUT")
6. Subscription option with additional savings
7. Free shipping threshold at $150+ (encourages bundle purchase)
8. FAQ section addressing objections (storage, testing, allergens)
9. Authority testimonials (Dr. Mark Hyman quote on collection page)
10. 60-day money-back guarantee

**Estimated Metrics:** AOV $100-$150, subscription rate 30-40%, LTV $300-$500.

### Recommended Funnel Architecture for Aluna (Supplements)

```
Meta Ad (UGC/Influencer) → Product/Bundle Page → Add to Cart → Checkout → Subscription Upsell
```

Key optimization: Display bundles first to drive AOV. Ensure review counts and star ratings are highly visible. Offer subscription with 15-20% savings.

### Recommended Funnel Architecture for Noho Labs (Telehealth)

```
Meta Ad (Education) → Symptom Quiz Landing Page → Personalized Protocol Recommendation → Book Consultation → Physician Visit → Rx + Fulfillment → Ongoing Subscription
```

Key optimization: Use the quiz to personalize the experience and qualify the lead before introducing the friction of a medical consultation. Show the personalized protocol recommendation before asking for payment.

## 8. Regulatory & Compliance Landscape

Navigating the FDA and Meta policy environments is the highest barrier to entry, but also the strongest competitive moat for brands that get it right.

### FDA Peptide Category System (Current as of June 2026)

| Category | Status | Compounding Eligibility | Key Peptides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category 1 | Under evaluation, sufficient safety data | MAY be compounded by 503A pharmacies | Sermorelin, GHK-Cu (non-injectable), others |
| Category 2 | Significant safety risks identified | CANNOT be compounded | BPC-157, TB-500, Epitalon, KPV, Semax, Selank, MOTS-C |
| Category 3 | Insufficient documentation | CANNOT be compounded | Various |

In late 2023, the FDA moved 19 widely used peptides to Category 2, effectively banning compounding pharmacies from preparing them. While HHS Secretary RFK Jr. stated in February 2026 his intention to move 14 of these back to Category 1, **no formal reclassification has occurred as of June 2026.**

### The 14 Peptides Discussed for Potential Reclassification

AOD-9604, BPC-157, Emideltide (DSIP), Epitalon, GHK-Cu, Kisspeptin-10, KPV, MOTS-C, Semax, Selank, TB-500, Thymosin Alpha-1, and 2 additional TBD.

**Critical distinction:** Reclassification to Category 1 only allows compounding while under review — it does NOT constitute FDA approval for safety or efficacy.

### Currently Legal for Advertising (Category 1 or Supplement)

| Compound | Status | Advertising Implications |
|---|---|---|
| Sermorelin | Category 1 (legal to compound) | Safe to advertise as Rx; requires age-gating |
| NAD+ | Legal as supplement AND compounded injectable | Supplement = no age-gate; Injectable = age-gated |
| Glutathione | Legal as supplement AND compounded | Same as NAD+ |
| GHK-Cu | Category 1 (non-injectable routes only) | Topical/oral = supplement positioning possible |
| Semaglutide | Complex (FDA vs. compounders legal battle ongoing) | High risk; monitor closely |

### Meta's 3-Tier Data Restriction System (November 2025)

| Tier | Restriction Level | Impact | Likely Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Standard restrictions | Limited custom audiences from health data | General wellness brands |
| Tier 2 | Enhanced restrictions | No optimization for purchases/leads via pixel; limited retargeting | Telehealth, peptide therapy |
| Tier 3 | Maximum restrictions | Severely limited targeting; no health-related custom audiences | Prescription drugs, sensitive conditions |

### Meta Advertising Policy Rules for Peptide/Telehealth Brands

**Prescription Drug Advertising:** Promoting prescription drugs is NOT allowed without Meta authorization. Eligibility is limited to online pharmacies, telehealth providers, and pharmaceutical manufacturers. Must apply for and receive special authorization. Age-gating is required.

**Supplement Advertising:** Dietary supplements can be advertised to 18+ without special authorization. Cannot imply negative self-perception, declare a "perfect body type," or use before/after comparisons for weight loss. No close-ups on specific body areas.

### Age-Gating Impact Analysis

| Positioning | Age-Gate Required? | Reach Impact | Pixel Optimization |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplement (Aluna) | No | Maximum reach | Tier 1-2 restrictions |
| Telehealth/Rx (Noho Labs) | Yes | Significantly reduced | Tier 2-3 restrictions |

This is the single most important strategic consideration for your dual-brand approach. Aluna as a supplement brand avoids age-gating entirely, allowing maximum reach and better pixel optimization.

### Compliance Checklist (Pre-Launch)

1. Confirm product/service is legally available in target states
2. Verify peptide is Category 1 or supplement
3. Apply for Meta prescription drug advertising authorization (Noho Labs)
4. Set up age-gating for Rx-related ads
5. Review all copy for prohibited health claims
6. Ensure "supports" / "helps maintain" language throughout
7. Set up Conversions API (server-side tracking) for Tier 2 restrictions
8. Use intermediary domain if needed to avoid health-category pixel restrictions
9. Document compliance review process for audit trail
