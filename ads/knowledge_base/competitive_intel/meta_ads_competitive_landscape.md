# Meta Ads Competitive Landscape: Peptide & Longevity Space
**Prepared for:** Noho Labs & Aluna Science
**Date:** June 16, 2026

## Executive Summary

The Meta advertising landscape for peptides and longevity treatments is highly fragmented, presenting a significant opportunity for Noho Labs and Aluna Science. While the GLP-1 weight loss market is heavily saturated by massive telehealth players like Hims and Ro, the broader peptide therapy space (including Sermorelin, BPC-157, and NAD+) lacks a dominant, compliant advertiser. 

The most successful brands in this space are currently bypassing strict medical claims by marketing liposomal supplements rather than prescription peptides, or by utilizing extensive influencer "whitelisting" networks to distribute risk and scale their reach. For Noho Labs, the clearest path to market dominance involves adopting the influencer whitelisting model pioneered by Rho Nutrition, while positioning Aluna Science as a premium, science-backed protocol brand similar to Cymbiotika or Magnali Health.

## Tier 1 Competitor Deep Dives

The most sophisticated advertisers in the longevity space are deploying advanced media buying strategies and premium brand positioning. 

### Rho Nutrition (The Whitelisting Pioneer)
Rho Nutrition is currently the most aggressive and sophisticated advertiser in the liposomal peptide and longevity supplement space, running approximately 910 active ads. 

Their primary strategy relies on **influencer whitelisting**. Instead of running all advertisements through the official Rho Nutrition Facebook page, they run identical creative assets through the verified pages of their influencer partners. These partners include functional medicine doctors (Dr. Becky Campbell), fitness personalities (Michael Chernow, Mind Pump), and professional athletes (Laird Hamilton, Lauren Fisher). 

Their creative approach relies heavily on user-generated content (UGC) video testimonials from these influencers. The messaging consistently emphasizes "Liposomal Technology" for superior absorption, directly addressing the common consumer complaint that traditional supplements are ineffective. They frequently bundle products into protocols, such as the "Cellular Longevity Stack" (NAD+ + Glutathione + Curcumin), which increases average order value. 

### Cymbiotika (The Premium Benchmark)
Cymbiotika positions itself at the absolute premium end of the market, focusing on clinical validation rather than influencer hype. Their core advertising message is, "The industry talks about results. We actually measure them."

They differentiate themselves by running independent clinical trials on their finished products, not just individual ingredients. Their ads frequently highlight these specific data points, such as claiming a 50% increase in glutathione levels over baseline from a single dose. Furthermore, they leverage their retail presence in Ulta Beauty as a form of offline social proof to validate their online advertising. They also partner with high-profile biohackers like Gary Brecka to lend credibility to their formulations.

### Hims & Ro (The GLP-1 Giants)
The telehealth giants dominate the GLP-1 and weight loss segments with massive ad spend. Due to Meta's strict health policies, virtually all of their advertisements are age-gated, requiring users to log in to view the content. 

Their strategy is built on high-volume, multi-variant testing. They run dozens of different creative variations simultaneously, constantly iterating to find winning combinations. However, this aggressive approach carries risks; recent advertisements from the Zimmerman Reed law firm indicate that Hims and Hers are currently facing legal investigation regarding their compounded GLP-1 marketing claims.

## Dominant Creative and Messaging Strategies

Successful campaigns in the peptide and longevity space share several common characteristics regarding format and messaging.

**User-Generated Content (UGC) is the dominant format.** Approximately 60% to 70% of successful advertisements utilize UGC video. The most effective variations feature doctors, pharmacists, or functional medicine practitioners explaining the science behind the products, rather than simply pitching the benefits.

**Education precedes the sale.** The highest-performing brands do not lead with a hard sell. Instead, they educate the consumer on cellular aging. For example, successful NAD+ campaigns explain that traditional NAD+ injections are inefficient, and that the body requires precursors like NMN to convert into NAD+ intracellularly. This education-first approach builds trust and justifies premium pricing.

**Selling protocols, not pills.** Brands are moving away from selling individual supplements and toward marketing comprehensive systems. Examples include Magnali Health's "first complete aging system" and Rho Nutrition's "Total Rho Stack." This strategy not only increases revenue but also improves patient outcomes by combining synergistic compounds.

**The "Anti-DIY" positioning.** Legitimate telehealth providers are beginning to differentiate themselves from the gray market of "research peptides." Clinics like Halo Concierge Doctors run advertisements warning consumers against taking peptide advice from influencers or podcasts, emphasizing that peptide therapy requires professional medical supervision.

## Strategic Recommendations for Noho Labs & Aluna

Based on this competitive analysis, Noho Labs and Aluna Science should implement the following strategies for their Meta Ads launch.

1. **Exploit the Sermorelin and BPC-157 Voids.** While GLP-1 is saturated, the markets for Sermorelin and BPC-157 are virtually uncontested by sophisticated advertisers. Noho Labs should prioritize these compounds in initial campaigns, as the cost to acquire a customer (CAC) will likely be significantly lower due to decreased auction competition.

2. **Implement an Influencer Whitelisting Strategy.** Aluna Science should immediately begin recruiting functional medicine practitioners, fitness professionals, and wellness influencers. Running advertisements through their verified Meta pages (as Rho Nutrition does) will bypass the inherent distrust consumers have for new health brands and rapidly scale social proof.

3. **Adopt "Education-First" Video Creative.** Do not launch with static images of vials or pill bottles. Invest in high-quality UGC videos where experts explain the mechanism of action—such as how peptides signal collagen production or how NAD+ supports mitochondrial function. 

4. **Prepare for Age-Gating.** Assume that any advertisement mentioning prescription peptides or specific medical outcomes will be age-gated by Meta. Ensure that the creative hook in the first three seconds is compelling enough to convince a user to log in and verify their age to continue watching.

5. **Position Against the "Research-Only" Market.** Noho Labs can build immediate trust by explicitly contrasting its medically supervised, pharmacy-compounded treatments against the unregulated "research-use-only" peptide suppliers currently flooding the market. Emphasize safety, purity, and physician oversight as core brand pillars.
