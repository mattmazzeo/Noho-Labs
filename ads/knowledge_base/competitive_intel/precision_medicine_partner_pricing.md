## Competitive Implication for NAD+
The most important observed benchmark is **NAD+ 200mg/mL, 5mL at $58.00** from the outside pharmacy partner. This suggests a telehealth brand buying from this partner would likely land near the following rough economics before clinician, CAC, operations, and payment processing:

| Item | Estimated Amount |
|---|---:|
| Pharmacy acquisition cost (partner price) | $58 |
| Shipping/cold chain/admin estimate | $10–$20 |
| Approximate delivered cost before marketing | $68–$78 |

That means a brand charging **$79 for month 1** is likely operating at minimal gross profit or a loss once non-pharmacy costs are included, and a brand charging **$149 ongoing** is using month 2+ pricing to recover acquisition and pharmacy costs.

## Strategic Interpretation
For Aluna / Noho, this partner proposal reinforces that competitor promo pricing is likely a customer acquisition tactic rather than a sustainably profitable everyday price. It also confirms the structural advantage of owning the pharmacy or controlling compounding economics directly, especially for NAD+, glutathione, and sermorelin.
