### Ad Longevity Patterns:
- **Rho Nutrition:** Bulk launched Feb 13, 2026 (suggests major campaign reset). Ads running 4+ months = proven winners.
- **Most supplement brands:** 30-60 day ad lifespan before creative refresh
- **Telehealth brands:** Shorter cycles (2-4 weeks) due to compliance pressure

### Platform Distribution:
- All major advertisers run across Facebook + Instagram + Messenger + Audience Network
- Instagram Reels/Stories appear to be primary placement for video UGC
- Facebook Feed for static/carousel product ads

## 7. Competitive Gaps & Opportunities:

### Under-Advertised Categories (Low Competition):
1. **Sermorelin** - Only ~2,600 ads, 3 visible advertisers
2. **BPC-157** - Only ~1,800 ads, mostly research suppliers
3. **Peptide therapy (general)** - Fragmented across local clinics, no national DTC brand
4. **GHK-Cu** - Breakout search trend, minimal advertising
5. **Tesamorelin** - Growing interest, very few advertisers

### Over-Saturated Categories (High Competition):
1. **GLP-1/weight loss** - 42K+ ads, dominated by well-funded players
2. **NAD+ supplements** - 42K+ ads, Rho dominates with influencer strategy
3. **General glutathione** - 8K+ ads, crowded

### Whitespace for Noho Labs / Aluna:
- **Peptide therapy education** (explain what peptides are, build category)
- **Multi-peptide protocols** (stack positioning like Rho but for Rx peptides)
- **Sermorelin + growth hormone** (very low competition)
- **BPC-157 for recovery** (athletes, post-surgery, gut health)
- **Longevity-focused telehealth** (differentiate from weight-loss-only players)
