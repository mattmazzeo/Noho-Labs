## Hook Formulas That Work (Observed from Top Performers)

### Category 1: Problem-Agitation Hooks
These hooks lead with a symptom or frustration the audience already feels:

1. **"Most supplements don't absorb well, which is why people don't feel results."** (Rho/Mind Pump)
2. **"My brain fog completely vanished!"** (Cymbiotika - testimonial hook)
3. **"I feel 10 years younger!"** (Cymbiotika - transformation hook)
4. **"I used to piece together supplements from different brands."** (Rho/Dr. London)

### Category 2: Authority/Expert Hooks
These hooks leverage credibility of the speaker:

1. **"Why I Take Glutathione Every Day"** (Rho/Dr. Francesca LeBlanc)
2. **"They call glutathione the master antioxidant for a reason."** (Cymbiotika/Gary Brecka)
3. **"Over the past decade, our team of PhD chemists developed a technology..."** (Rho)
4. **"I'm picky with supplements, especially anything I take daily."** (Cymbiotika/joyjah)

### Category 3: Newsworthiness/Discovery Hooks
These hooks create curiosity about something new:

1. **"JUST LAUNCHED: Liposomal Collagen Peptides"** (Rho)
2. **"Your next Ulta run just got a wellness upgrade."** (Cymbiotika)
3. **"Introducing The Total Rho Stack, our most comprehensive bundle yet"** (Rho)

### Category 4: Social Proof Hooks
These hooks leverage numbers and validation:

1. **"100+ Million Packets Sold"** (Cymbiotika)
2. **"50,000+ Reviews"** (Cymbiotika)
3. **"500,000+ customers"** (Rho - implied through reviews)
4. **"Real people, real results, real science behind every packet."** (Cymbiotika)

### Category 5: Urgency/Offer Hooks
These hooks create time pressure:

1. **"20% OFF Everything for the next 24 hours only!"** (Rho)
2. **"Code: FOUNDER -- Don't wait, this is a limited time offer"** (Rho)
3. **"Up to 25% Off Your First Rho Order"** (Rho)

## Messaging Architecture Patterns

### Pattern 1: The "Science + Simplicity" Framework (Rho Nutrition)
**Structure:** Complex science → Simple benefit → Social proof → CTA

Example:
> "Our products are highly effective because of our Liposomal Delivery Technology. Over the past decade, our team of PhD chemists developed a technology that encapsulates active ingredients—like NAD+, Curcumin, and Glutathione—in microscopic protective membranes called liposomes designed to support absorption."

**Why it works:** Establishes credibility through scientific language, then makes it accessible.

### Pattern 2: The "Routine Integration" Framework (Rho/Influencers)
**Structure:** "I added X to my routine" → Specific use case → Felt difference → Recommendation

Example (Michael Chernow):
> "I recently added Rho Nutrition to my daily routine. Every morning, I make a water infused cocktail with Rho Curcumin + Resveratrol, NAD+ and Glutathione. This is my Cellular Longevity Stack! The cool thing about Rho is that all their supplements use Liposomal Technology... I absolutely feel the difference!"

**Why it works:** Makes the product feel like a natural addition, not a dramatic change.

### Pattern 3: The "Problem-Solution-Mechanism" Framework (Cymbiotika)
**Structure:** Common problem → Product as solution → Why it works differently → Proof

Example:
> "Daily wellness starts with digestion. Irish Sea Moss supports gut health, skin clarity, and energy as you age."

**Why it works:** Connects a universal concern to a specific product benefit.

### Pattern 4: The "Stack/Protocol" Framework (Rho)
**Structure:** Multiple products → Combined benefit → Savings → Bundle CTA

Example:
> "Introducing The Total Rho Stack—featuring 10 essential products plus the Rho Serving Spoon, all pre-discounted at 20% off, saving you $84! Fuel your body with the Total Rho Stack. This ultimate bundle features 11 essentials, including Creatine, NAD+, Collagen, Glutathione, and more for energy, recovery, immunity, and peak performance."

**Why it works:** Drives AOV up, positions brand as complete solution, creates switching cost.

## Compliance Language Patterns (How Winners Stay Within Meta Policy)

### Words/Phrases USED (Compliant):
- "Designed to support..." (not "treats" or "cures")
- "Supports cellular health"
- "Clinically proven to reduce wrinkles over 20% in 90 days" (with clinical backing)
- "Supports immune health, detox pathways"
- "Promotes energy metabolism"
- "Support hormone metabolism"
- "Enhanced absorption"
- "Superior bioavailability"
- "60-day money-back guarantee" (risk reversal without health claims)

### Words/Phrases AVOIDED:
- No "cure," "treat," "heal," "fix"
- No specific disease claims (no "diabetes," "cancer," "Alzheimer's")
- No before/after body images
- No "FDA approved" (for supplements)
- No "prescription" or "drug" language in supplement ads
- No "injection" or "needle" imagery in non-age-gated ads

### Age-Gating Observations:
- AgelessRx: ALL ads are age-gated (requires login to view)
- Rho Nutrition: NO age-gating (supplement positioning avoids it)
- Cymbiotika: NO age-gating (supplement positioning)
- Sermorelin advertisers: ALL age-gated
- Implication: **Positioning as supplement vs. prescription dramatically affects ad reach**

## Visual Creative Patterns

### Video Ad Structure (UGC - Most Common):
1. **0-3 seconds:** Hook (face-to-camera, bold statement or question)
2. **3-10 seconds:** Problem identification or personal story
3. **10-25 seconds:** Product introduction + mechanism of action
4. **25-40 seconds:** Personal results or benefits experienced
5. **40-60 seconds:** CTA (discount code, link, urgency)

### Static Ad Structure:
1. **Hero product image** with clean background
2. **Bold headline** (benefit-focused, not feature-focused)
3. **Supporting copy** (1-2 lines of mechanism/proof)
4. **CTA button** (Shop Now, Learn More, See Details)
5. **Trust badge** (money-back guarantee, review count)

### Color & Design Patterns:
- **Rho Nutrition:** Clean white/cream backgrounds, amber/gold product bottles, minimalist
- **Cymbiotika:** Dark/luxury aesthetic, gold accents, premium feel
- **AgelessRx:** Clinical blue/white, medical trust signals
- **Kind Patches:** Bright, energetic colors, lifestyle imagery

## Key Takeaways for Noho Labs / Aluna:

### What to Replicate:
1. **Influencer whitelisting** is the #1 strategy in this space (Rho: 10+ pages, Cymbiotika: Gary Brecka)
2. **Liposomal/absorption messaging** is the dominant differentiator claim
3. **Bundle/stack positioning** drives AOV and creates switching costs
4. **UGC video from credible experts** outperforms polished brand content
5. **"Supports" language** keeps you compliant while still being persuasive

### Where to Differentiate:
1. **Prescription peptide education** — nobody is doing this well on Meta
2. **Protocol-based positioning** — "Your personalized peptide protocol" vs. one-size-fits-all
3. **Physician-led content** — more authoritative than influencer testimonials for Rx products
4. **Concierge/white-glove service** — premium positioning vs. mass-market supplement brands
5. **Results tracking/data** — show measurable outcomes (labs, biomarkers) vs. subjective testimonials

### Creative Volume Benchmarks:
- To compete at Rho's level: 50-100 new creatives/month
- Minimum viable: 10-20 new creatives/month
- Testing cadence: 3-5 new hooks per week
- Kill threshold: <1% CTR after $50 spend = kill the ad
