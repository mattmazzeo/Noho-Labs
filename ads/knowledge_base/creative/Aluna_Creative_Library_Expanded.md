## SECTION E: Testing Cadence & Kill Criteria

### Week 1: Launch Burst
Launch 10 ads simultaneously (5 physician + 5 UGC). Budget: $50-$100/day total ($5-$10 per ad).

### Day 3-4: First Kill Round
Pause any ad with:
- CTR below 0.8%
- CPM above $25
- Thumbstop rate below 25%

### Day 5-7: Double Down
Take top 3-4 performers. Create 3 hook variations of each (using the Hook Bank above). Launch as new ads.

### Week 2: Format Diversification
Take the winning hook + script. Reproduce in a different format (e.g., if talking head won, try text-on-screen version of same script).

### Week 3: Scale
Move proven winners (7+ days of consistent performance) into the "Scale" campaign at 70-80% of total budget. Continue testing new concepts in "Sandbox" at 20-30%.

### Ongoing: 2-4 New Concepts Per Week
Creative fatigue sets in after 7-14 days at scale. Maintain a constant pipeline of fresh creative to prevent performance decay.
